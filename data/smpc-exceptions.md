# SmPC source exceptions

Last reviewed: 2026-09-22

The previous exception list has been substantially resolved using current Irish medicines.ie sources. Direct Irish SmPCs are now identified for Plaquenil, Salazopyrin, CellCept, Myfortic, Neoral, Kineret, Bimzelx, Stelara, Tremfya and Nucala.

## Remaining source exception

- Sarilumab — Kevzara: a direct current Irish medicines.ie SmPC URL has not yet been confirmed in this pass. Use an explicitly labelled EMA product-information fallback if the Irish source remains unavailable after direct verification.

## Rule

Do not infer clinical dosing from another brand solely because the active ingredient is the same. A representative SmPC may cover clinically equivalent presentations only after route/formulation equivalence is established. Device-specific administration remains separate.
